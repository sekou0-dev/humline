# App Review Notes — Humline

The level geometry is the melody; the microphone is the instrument.

Humline is not a tap-to-fly game with a microphone skin. Each phrase is authored as a sequence of notes. That sequence is extruded into a pitch corridor: a held note is a valley, a leap of a fifth is a cliff. The player must hum (or whistle) to stay inside the written line. Silence stalls the craft.

Microphone audio is processed on device for pitch only and is never uploaded.

How to play in review:

1. Grant microphone access. The permission copy states the mic is the controller.
2. Complete the short low/high calibration, or skip it if a previous calibration exists.
3. On Simulator, drag vertically to stand in for pitch.
4. Fly **First Phrase**. The hills on screen are the same shape as the rising and falling notes.

Throat-hum is the default input so the game can be played quietly.

Shared “Beat my flight” files are pitch envelopes (JSON), not audio recordings.

Product ID for the optional phrase pack: `com.catfordlabs.humline.phrasepack` (non-consumable). The first four phrases are free.
